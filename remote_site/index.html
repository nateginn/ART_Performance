New-Patient Counting — Read This First

Unique New Patients is the headline figure: each newly-arrived patient is counted exactly once, company-wide. New-Patient Visits counts every provider/category a new patient was actually seen by on their first day — it can be higher than Unique New Patients whenever one new patient saw more than one provider or category the same day. New-Patient Visits is never a count of unique patients and must never be summed across providers or categories and presented as such.
